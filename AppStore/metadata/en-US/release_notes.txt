New in 1.0.5
• New TV guide: on iPhone the selected channel plays live at the top, channels on the left and programs on the right, with NOW, catch-up and reminders
• On iPad and Mac a large live preview with Watch live and From the start, plus channel columns
• On Apple TV a full-width TV guide: the NOW card plays the channel live when you focus it, OK opens full screen
• Live TV now always opens in the TV guide; the List button switches to the list
• The live preview is muted and lets your music keep playing until you turn the sound on
• New setting Live preview in the guide: Always, Wi-Fi only or Never
• The TV guide only shows days that have guide information
