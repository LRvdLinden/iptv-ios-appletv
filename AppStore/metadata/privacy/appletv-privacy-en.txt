Bingr is a player for your own IPTV subscription and does not provide any content. Bingr requires an account: we store your name, email address and, encrypted, your profiles, servers, lists and progress, so Bingr works the same on all your devices. Per device we store a device ID, name, type and versions; while watching, which profile watches what (deleted after 8 days). No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
